The Man

I make films that hit like the first chorus.

Joe Laredo is a 21-year-old filmmaker from Bremen, Northern Germany. He writes, shoots and cuts his own work — raw, stylish, and always with a story behind the image.

From short films like Smoke Break to visuals for artists, he builds worlds that feel like cinema, whatever the budget.

Based in Bremen. Working internationally — Miami, LA and wherever the story is.
